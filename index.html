BUILT AROUND BVI

Your itinerary moves. Your calm travels with you.

Whether you are anchored near Virgin Gorda or staying in a hillside villa on Tortola, the right local details matter. Share your island, property or vessel, and we will review therapist coverage and safe access before confirming a session.

For villas, share the full address and gate details. For hotels, confirm outside-provider permission. For yachts, give the vessel name, marina or anchorage, and safe access arrangements.

Inter-island transfers and staffing are not assumed. Each island, yacht and hotel appointment requires separate access and therapist confirmation.
