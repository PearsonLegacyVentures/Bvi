Private massage near Road Town

Guests around Road Town may request massage at suitable villas, hotel suites, residences or yacht locations where access is approved. Yacht embarkation, short stays and marina access. A booking request is reviewed for therapist availability, site suitability and travel rather than treated as an automatic reservation.

Specify marina, berth and meeting point. For a private residence or villa, send the property name and exact entrance; for a resort, confirm outside-provider rules. For yacht guests, share the marina or anchorage and the captain's contact.

What can I request here?

Start with Swedish massage when you want a relaxation-focused session. Guests who prefer firmer work can explore deep tissue massage. Couples massage may be possible with two therapists and enough safe treatment space.

Special arrangements for villas, hotels and resorts, and yachts need a location-specific review.

How to plan the appointment

  1. Confirm your exact address and the name of the host, resort or vessel.
  2. Choose a treatment, duration and preferred local date and time.
  3. Provide gate, marina, security or parking instructions where relevant.
  4. Allow time after excursions and wait for confirmation before making payment.

Weather, traffic, transfers or private-property restrictions can affect whether the requested setting is viable. Inter-island transfers and staffing are not assumed. Each island, yacht and hotel appointment requires separate access and therapist confirmation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a same-day treatment in Road Town?

You can ask, but availability is not guaranteed. Therapist scheduling and travel requirements must be checked. Requests made in advance are easier to coordinate.

Is my hotel's spa the same as a private mobile service?

No. This is an enquiry for a Tranquilitas-arranged private service, subject to the hotel's policy regarding external providers. Do not assume your accommodation permits an outside therapist.

What if I am staying outside Road Town?

Use our destination service-area directory to identify the right area, then tell us the actual location. Some addresses may need a custom travel quote or may not be serviceable.
